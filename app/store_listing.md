# Store Listing Details

**App Name:** GPS Coordinates

**App Category & Purpose:** 
*Category:* Navigation / Tools
*Purpose:* A simple, efficient utility application designed to instantly fetch, display, and manage a user's exact global positioning (latitude and longitude). It solves the problem of needing quick, highly-accurate location data without the bloated overhead of a full mapping application.

**Key Features & Benefits:**
* **Instant GPS Fetching:** Immediately retrieve your current latitude and longitude using high-accuracy location services.
* **Save & Label Locations:** Locally store specific coordinates with custom labels (e.g., "Parked Car", "Home", "Hidden Trail") for future reference.
* **Pin Important Locations:** Pin your most frequently accessed or important saved coordinates to the top of your list.
* **Search & Filter:** Quickly find past locations using a real-time text search filter against your saved labels.
* **One-Tap Map Integration & Clipboard:** Copy raw coordinates to your clipboard instantly, or launch them directly into your preferred Maps application with a single tap.

**Target Audience:** 
Hikers, outdoor enthusiasts, drone pilots, delivery drivers, land surveyors, and everyday users who need precise, raw location data or a lightweight tool to save specific waypoints.

**Design Preferences:**
* **Theme:** "Deep Space / Radar" aesthetic.
* **Primary Colors:** Deep Navy Blue (`#0F172A`, `#1E1B4B`), Ocean Blue (`#0284C7`), and Bright Red (`#EF4444`) for contrast markers.
* **Iconography:** A circular logo featuring a 3D silver map pin with an integrated compass, emitting radar/satellite waves on a technical grid background.
* **UI Style:** Modern Jetpack Compose Material 3 design, prioritizing readability, high contrast, and rapid one-handed usability.
