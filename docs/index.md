# Privacy Policy for GPS Coordinates

**Last Updated:** October 11, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use GPS Coordinates ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. 

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our mobile application, **GPS Coordinates**, and any related services, sales, marketing, or events (we refer to them collectively in this Privacy Policy as the "Services").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully as it will help you make informed decisions about sharing your personal information with us.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

**Location Data:**
Our application's primary purpose is to fetch, display, and manage your current GPS coordinates. To provide this service, we request access to your device's precise and coarse location data (using standard Android Location Permissions).

**How Location Data is Used:**
* **Real-time Display:** We use this data strictly to show your current latitude and longitude within the app's interface.
* **Local Storage:** If you choose to save a location, the coordinates (along with a label of your choosing) are stored **locally** on your device using a local database (SQLite/Room).

### 2. Data Sharing and Cloud Storage

**We do not collect, transmit, store, or share your data on any external servers.** 
All saved locations, labels, and timestamps reside entirely on your physical device. We do not have access to your saved locations. We do not sell, rent, or trade your location data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics companies.

### 3. Third-Party Integrations

Our application allows you to export your coordinates via standard Android Intents (e.g., the "Map" button). When you use this feature, you are passing your location data to a third-party application of your choosing (such as Google Maps). 
Once the data is passed to a third-party application, their respective Privacy Policies govern the use of that data. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the mapping applications you use.

### 4. Data Retention

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain complete control over its retention:
* You can delete individual saved locations at any time directly within the app.
* If you uninstall the GPS Coordinates application or clear the app's data in your Android settings, all saved location history will be permanently deleted.

### 5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since the app functions entirely offline and does not transmit data to us, no data is collected from children by our systems.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.

### 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
